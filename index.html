Residential & Commercial Tiling

Alan Bush Tiling is your local tiling, waterproofing specialist in Tauranga and surrounding regions. Alan can take care of all your tiling needs from residential to commercial, interior and exterior.

Wall & Floor Tiling

From splashbacks to full floors, Alan lays wall and floor tiles with careful preparation and precise setting out, so every line runs straight and every tile sits true. Not sure which tile will suit your space? Alan is happy to talk you through the options.

Exterior Tiling

Tiling isn't just for indoors. Alan tiles exterior areas to stand up to New Zealand's weather, using the right products and waterproofing so your outdoor spaces look great and last for years.

  • Entrance ways
  • Paths
  • Exterior walls
  • Patios
  • Steps

Waterproofing

Proper waterproofing is what stands between a beautiful tiled space and costly water damage. As a qualified installer, Alan waterproofs and seals wet areas to code, giving you peace of mind that they are protected for the long term.

  • Showers
  • Bathrooms
  • Wet areas
  • Laundries
  • Kitchens

Kitchen Tiling

The kitchen is the heart of the home, and the right tiling ties the whole space together. From splashbacks to floors, Alan will help you achieve a hard-wearing, easy-to-clean finish that looks the part.

  • Walls
  • Floors
  • Splash backs

Bathroom & Laundry Tiling

Bathrooms and laundries are where quality tiling and waterproofing really matter. Alan installs wall and floor tiles, waterproofs the wet areas and finishes every detail so your space is both practical and beautiful.

Underfloor Heating

Warm tiles underfoot, right through winter. Alan supplies and installs Warmfloor underfloor heating — an efficient, NZ-designed system that turns cold tiled floors into cosy ones.

  • Ideal for most flooring surfaces
  • Quick heat up time
  • Easy to use thermostat
  • NZ-designed and manufactured cable
  • Available in a range of cable lengths
  • Healthy, clean and affordable

ABOUT

Hi, I'm Alan. I have 10 years experience as a tiler and started my own business Alan Bush Tiling Contractor back in 2017 after previously working for Mike's Tiling Services and ABC Contracting.

I am a member of TANZ (Tilers Association of New Zealand) and am an industry approved waterproofing applicator.

I have built my business on a solid reputation of affordable and quality tiling installation that has seen my business grow with a steady stream of repeat customers and referral work.

I specialise in all tiling aspects residential and commercial, indoor and outdoor. I can remove your old floorcoverings and replace them with new tiles. Whether you require a repair job or a new tiling job I'm here to help. I am skilled and knowledgeable in a wide variety of techniques and strive to help you achieve your desired outcome. In addition I have a number of qualifications and certifications for your added peace of mind.

  • TANZ (Tilers Association of New Zealand)
  • Ardex certified waterproof applicator
  • Bostik certified waterproof applicator
  • Davco certified waterproof applicator
  • Mapei certified waterproof applicator
  • Sika certified waterproof applicator
  • Wedi waterproof panel system installer/applicator
  • Warm Floor underfloor heating approved installer
